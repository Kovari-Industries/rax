# Place a folded towel on a pile

Read the `cloth-handling` skill. Use `Cloth.stack_folded` with a task perception
function, calibrated metric geometry, and measured tool settings. Identify the
folded packet footprint and grasps that capture all layers. Measure the pile's
available footprint, orientation, and current top height.

After the test lift, verify complete packet capture in both hands. Measure the
packet and pile again. Lift before transfer. Keep the hand span during a turn.
Verify support and alignment before opening the fingers. Retract and inspect the
released towel and the pile. Stop on uncertain layers, height, fit, or support.

Report the final observed state and evidence paths. Do not claim success from
finger position or motion completion alone. Physical tracking and cloth handling
must be qualified separately from mock-controller tests.
