**Start.** You start standing between two identical metal packing
tables whose long sides run parallel to your initial heading. A hammer, with a wooden (yellow-orange)
handle and a dark metal head, lies flat on the table to your right. The basket, an open-topped dark
grey plastic crate, stands on the table to your left. A long, low white bar lies on the floor between
the two tables.

**Randomization.** The hammer's position and the basket's orientation are randomized.

**Success.** The hammer's centre of mass must be inside the basket. That means inside the
basket's outline seen from above, and between the basket's floor and its rim. The episode
ends at the first moment this holds. You need not let go of the hammer. A hammer on the
floor never counts.
