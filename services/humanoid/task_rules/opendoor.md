**Start.** You start standing and facing a wall that runs left to
right across your path, with a door in it. As you face it, the door leaf is hinged on its left edge
and swings away from you. A lever handle on both faces of the leaf sits towards
its free edge.

**Randomization.** The door assembly's position is randomized.

**Success.** Three conditions must hold at the same moment:

- You are standing.
- The latch has been released. Pressing the lever down by about 20 degrees releases it,
  and it then stays released for the rest of the episode.
- The leaf is open at least 60 degrees from closed.

The episode ends at the first moment all three hold.

While latched, the leaf is held firmly closed. Once released it is heavily damped: it moves
slowly while you push, and drifts slowly back towards closed when you stop.
