**Start.** You start standing and facing a metal packing table that
runs left to right across your path. A brown cardboard box with tape stands on the table. The shelf
is not in front of you: a steel shelving unit with blue uprights and dark shelf boards, on a low
white plinth, stands to your left, with one long side facing you across the gap. It holds
cardboard cartons on its lower and upper levels; the target level is the clear board between them.

**Randomization.** The box and shelf positions and orientations are randomized.

**Success.** Two conditions must hold at the same moment:

- The box's bottom is within about 6 cm of the target shelf level.
- The box's centre is over the shelf, at least about 10 cm inside the shelf's outline.

The episode ends at the first moment both hold. The box need not be at rest, and you need not
let go.
