**Start.** You start standing, facing two punching bags that hang on
chains from stands: a black bag ahead on your left and a red bag ahead on your right. The target is
a small bright green ball on the side of one bag that faces you.

**Randomization.** The target's bag and position are randomized.

**Success.** The centre of either palm must come within around 0.15 m of the green target's
centre. The wrist does not count, and the speed of the hand does not matter. The episode
ends at the first moment this holds.
