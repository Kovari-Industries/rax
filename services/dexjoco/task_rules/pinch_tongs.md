**Goal:** Pick up the tongs and squeeze them shut and open again three times in a row.

**Success requires, held for a number of consecutive steps:**
- The tongs are held a set height above the table.
- At least three pinches have been counted. A pinch counts when the tongs close past a set angle after having been opened past another; the first one needs an opening first.

**Scene variation:** the tongs' position and the table height change between layouts. The tongs start open and spring back open when released.
