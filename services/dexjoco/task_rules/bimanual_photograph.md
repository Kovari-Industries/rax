**Goal:** Pick up the camera with the left hand, aim it at the logo and press the shutter button with the right hand.

**Success requires, at the same step:**
- The camera's lens is inside a target region in front of the logo.
- The camera points at the logo within a set angle.
- Something other than the camera itself presses the shutter button.

**Scene variation:** the logo's position on the wall and the camera's position change between layouts.
