**Goal:** Pick up the hammer and drive the nail into the wooden board.

**Success requires:**
- The nail is driven to a set depth into the board. The nail only goes down when the hammer's head strikes it while moving downward fast enough; each such strike drives it a little further, more for a faster strike, up to a cap.

**Scene variation:** the hammer's position and heading, the nail's position on the board and the table height change between layouts.
