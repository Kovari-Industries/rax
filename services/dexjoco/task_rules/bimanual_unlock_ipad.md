**Goal:** Pick up the iPad and enter the password 1, 2, 3 to unlock it.

**Success requires, held for a number of consecutive steps:**
- The digits 1, 2 and 3 were pressed in this order and the screen shows unlocked. A digit is pressed when a fingertip or the thumb tip of the right hand newly touches its button; the left hand's fingers do not press buttons.

**Fails the episode if at any step:**
- A wrong digit is pressed (the episode ends at once).

**Scene variation:** the stand's position (the iPad sits on it) and the table height change between layouts.
