**Goal:** Pick up the watering can and water the plant.

**Success requires:**
- The can's nozzle is within a set distance of the plant (horizontally and vertically) while the trigger is pulled, for a number of consecutive steps. The trigger counts as pulled past a set travel and released below a lower one.

**Fails the episode if at any step:**
- The trigger is pulled while the nozzle is outside that region around the plant.

**Scene variation:** the can's and the plant's positions and the table height change between layouts.
