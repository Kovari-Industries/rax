**Goal:** Place the digit tiles on the row of pads, left to right, so they form the largest possible number, and return both arms to their starting poses.

**Success requires, all at the same step:**
- The digits sit in descending order, each close to the centre of its pad.
- Each digit is readable the right way up and not turned far (eight and zero are checked more loosely).
- Both arms are back near their starting poses.

**Partial credit (failed episodes):** one more digit correctly placed per tier, up to one short of all; each tier also needs open grippers.
