**Goal:** Move all four eggs from the basket into the egg holder, close its lid, and return both arms to their starting poses.

**Success requires, all at the same step:**
- All four eggs rest down in the holder (an egg on top of the lid does not count).
- The lid is closed, or very nearly.
- Both arms are back near their starting poses.

**Partial credit (failed episodes):** one, two, three, then all four eggs in the holder; each tier also needs open grippers.
