**Goal:** Put the pliers, hammer, tape measure and wrench each into its matching slot in the toolbox, and return both arms to their starting poses.

**Success requires, all at the same step:**
- Each tool is roughly at its own slot and closely matches the slot's orientation (the pliers may lie either face up).
- Each tool lies fully inside the toolbox, not sticking out over its rim.
- Both arms are back near their starting poses.

**Partial credit (failed episodes):** one, then two, then three tools stored at the same time, ignoring orientation; each tier also needs mostly open grippers.
