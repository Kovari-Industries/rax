**Goal:** Slide the three cubes into one straight row using the long bar, without lifting any cube, and return both arms to their starting poses.

**Success requires, all at the same step:**
- The three cubes lie closely on one straight line, in any direction.
- Each cube is squared to the row, not turned diagonally.
- Both arms are back near their starting poses.

**Fails the episode if at any step:**
- Any cube rises even slightly above its starting height (lifted, tipped or stacked).
