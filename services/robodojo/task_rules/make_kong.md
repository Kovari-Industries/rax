**Goal:** Wait for the opponent arm to knock over one of its tiles, lay your three matching hand tiles face up, then stand the loose wall tile at the right end of your hand.

**Success requires, in order:**
1. All at the same step:
   - Your three tiles matching the discarded tile lie face up.
   - Your other triplet tiles still stand very nearly upright.
   - The loose wall tile is still face down.
2. All at the same step, later:
   - The previous stage's face-up and standing conditions still hold.
   - The loose wall tile stands very nearly upright, closely at the next spot past the right end of your hand.
   - Both grippers are mostly open.

**Fails the episode if at any step:** your arms move far from their starting poses while the opponent arm is away from its own.
