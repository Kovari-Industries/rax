**Goal:** Watch the demonstrator arm put five objects into the far basket, then put your matching five objects into your basket in the same order and return both arms to their starting poses.

**Success requires, in order:**
1. The first object of the sequence is in your basket and the rest are not.
2. The first two are in your basket and the rest are not.
3. The first three are in your basket and the rest are not.
4. The first four are in your basket and the last is not.
5. All five are in your basket and both arms are back near their starting poses.
- At every stage, all five demo objects are still in the far basket.

**Fails the episode if at any step:** your arms move far from their starting poses while the demonstrator arm is away from its own, or an object rests in your basket out of order while both grippers are open.

**Partial credit (failed episodes):** one, two, three, then four objects placed in order; each tier also needs open grippers.
