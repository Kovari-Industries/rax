**Goal:** Pick up the key, insert it upright into the lock block's keyhole, and turn it.

**Success requires, all at the same step:**
- The key is pushed fully into the keyhole, centred precisely.
- The key stands very nearly upright.
- The key is turned clockwise, seen from above, by somewhat less than a quarter turn relative to the lock block; the other way does not count.

**Partial credit (failed episodes):** the key lifted clearly off the table.
