**Goal:** Put all ten stacking pieces onto their matching pegs of the stacking base, then return both arms to their starting poses.

**Success requires, all at the same step:**
- The four identical pieces are stacked on the first peg, the three on the second, the two on the third and the single piece on the fourth.
- Each stack is lined up precisely over its peg.
- Every piece sits down on the base, not resting above it.
- Both arms are back near their starting poses.

**Partial credit (failed episodes):** one, two, then three peg groups complete; each tier also needs open grippers.
