**Goal:** Pick up the mallet and strike all eight xylophone bars in order, from the longest bar to the shortest.

**Success requires, in order:**
1. The mallet head strikes the longest bar, just above its top.
2. The mallet lifts clearly away from that strike.
3. The mallet strikes the next bar, then lifts clearly away; repeat for each following bar in order.
4. The mallet strikes the shortest bar.
- Only the next bar in order counts; hitting other bars is ignored, not penalised.
