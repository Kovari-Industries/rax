**Goal:** Thread each loose hex nut onto the fixed bolt of the matching colour, release it, and return both arms to their starting poses.

**Success requires, all at the same step:**
- Each of the three nuts lies flat, centred almost exactly on its own colour's bolt, and pushed some way down the shank.
- Both grippers are nearly fully open.
- Both arms are back near their starting poses.
