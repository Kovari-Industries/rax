**Goal:** Swap the two cubes between their mats in three moves using the empty mat, pressing the button once after each move, and return both arms to their starting poses.

**Success requires, in order:**
1. A cube is lifted from its starting mat.
2. A cube is placed on the empty mat.
3. The button is pressed.
4. The button is released.
5. A cube is lifted again.
6. The other cube is placed on the first cube's starting mat.
7. The button is pressed.
8. The button is released.
9. A cube is lifted again.
10. Each cube rests on the other's starting mat.
11. The button is pressed.
12. The button is released and both arms are back near their starting poses.

**Counted exactly:** the button is pressed exactly three times; an extra press fails the episode at once.

**Partial credit (failed episodes):** none.
