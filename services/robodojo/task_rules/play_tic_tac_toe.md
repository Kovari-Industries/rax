**Goal:** Play first against a scripted opponent arm, placing one piece per turn and returning both arms after each, until all nine cells are filled.

**Success requires, all at the same step:**
- All nine pieces (five yours, four the opponent's) sit centred on cells, resting on the board.

**Fails the episode if at any step:** the opponent arm and either of your arms have both moved far from their starting poses.

**Partial credit (failed episodes):** one, then two, then three, then four of your pieces on cells; each tier also needs open grippers.
