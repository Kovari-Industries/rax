**Goal:** Press the left red button as many times as its card shows, the blue button once, the middle red button as many times as its card shows, the blue button once more, then return both arms to their starting poses.

**Success requires, in order:**
1. The left red button pressed and released as many times as its card shows.
2. The blue button pressed and released once.
3. The middle red button pressed and released as many times as its card shows.
4. The blue button pressed, then released with both arms back near their starting poses.

**Counted exactly:** each button's presses must match its target; one press too many fails the episode at once.
