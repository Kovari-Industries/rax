**Goal:** Build a three-level tower (long board on two pillars, two more pillars carrying the medium board, then the small board and the top block) and return both arms to their starting poses.

**Success requires, all at the same step:**
- All eight blocks stand upright in their starting orientation.
- The long board rests on top of one left-pair and one right-pair pillar.
- One left-pair and one right-pair pillar stand on the long board, and the medium board rests on top of them, centred over the long board.
- The small board sits on the medium board and the top block on the small board, each closely centred and roughly aligned with the piece below.
- Both arms are back near their starting poses.

**Partial credit (failed episodes):** base level built and still, then the middle level built, upright and still; each tier also needs open grippers.
