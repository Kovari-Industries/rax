**Goal:** Put two of the four bread slices into the toaster, one per slot, press the lever down, and return both arms to their starting poses.

- Each toaster slot holds a slice, resting inside it.
- All four slices stand on edge, and the other two are still on the shelf.

- Both arms are back near their starting poses.
