**Goal:** Fold the long-sleeved top: both sleeves across the chest, then the hem up to the shoulders, then release and return both arms to their starting poses.

**Success requires:** judged at the moment both grippers open and both arms are back near their starting poses:
- Each sleeve's end lies roughly on the opposite side of the chest.
- Each hem corner lies roughly at the same side's shoulder, with the hem roughly parallel to the shoulders.

**Fails the episode if at any step:**
- An arm returns home while the body is folded but a sleeve is not.

**Partial credit (failed episodes):** both sleeves folded at a moment the grippers open.
